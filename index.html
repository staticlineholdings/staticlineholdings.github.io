01 · The Holding Company

One parent company. Room to grow.

Static Line Holdings Inc. was structured from day one as a holding company — a disciplined parent that owns and operates its businesses, holds their intellectual property, and directs strategy, finances, and compliance from the center while each company runs its own day-to-day operations.

The name comes from the founder's service as a paratrooper: on a static line jump, every jumper hooks into the same cable and trusts the system to deploy in sequence. That's how this company is built — each business hooks into one structure: one standard of accountability, one back office, one point of responsibility at the top.

The portfolio starts with two companies. The federal medical supply division is the growth engine — a veteran-owned wholesaler supplying medical and surgical products to VA and DoD customers, built on a lean structure and a nationwide supplier network. 9 Line Tattoo® is the established operating company — a veteran-owned studio in Riverview, Florida, operating since 2019, whose federal trademark is held by Static Line Holding Inc.

The structure is deliberately expandable. New divisions and acquisitions slot in under the same parent — same compliance, same standards, same single point of contact — without rebuilding the company around them.

  • Owns & operates its companies

    Static Line is the parent of record for its divisions and operating companies — ownership, accountability, and final decisions sit at the holding level.

  • Holds the intellectual property

    The company holds the federal trademark "9 LINE TATTOO" (Reg. No. 8398935) and the brand assets of its portfolio companies.

  • Directs strategy & compliance centrally

    Strategy, finances, registrations, and federal compliance — including SAM.gov registration and NAICS alignment — are managed once, at the parent level, for every company under it.
